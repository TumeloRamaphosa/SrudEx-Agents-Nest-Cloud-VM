## The Resource

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total methane potential | **700 MW** |
| Currently extracted | ~80 MW |
| Rwanda national grid | ~470 MW |
| Data centre target | **5-10 MW** |
| Carbon profile | Carbon-neutral (methane would be released naturally) |

## How It Works

1. Methane is extracted from Lake Kivu
2. Piped to a dedicated power plant
3. Powers a mini-grid independent of the national grid
4. Cheaper and more reliable than diesel
5. No carbon penalty — methane would escape naturally anyway

## Why It Matters

- A **dedicated methane mini-grid** can power a 5-10 MW AI data centre
- Independent of national grid reliability issues
- Attractive for ESG-conscious investors
- Positions Rwanda as a green AI hub
