# 🇷🇼 Rwanda Game Plan

> **From the Land of a Thousand Hills to Africa's AI Innovation Hub**
> Studex Group · August 2026

---

## Strategic Thesis

Rwanda is the ideal **compact flagship** for the Studex ecosystem — small enough to pilot end-to-end, progressive enough to enable fast execution, and strategically positioned as the neutral hub for East African deal-making.

## The Four-Layer Stack

### 1. 🎮 Arcade (Talent Pipeline)
- School/university gaming programs (low capex, high visibility)
- Mobile-first tournaments (EA Sports FC, Tekken)
- Weekly Sunday finals — Best Pitch wins a VM, Best Player announced
- 2 finalists per week get VMs + mentorship

### 2. 🌙 Midnight Founders Club (Startup Conversion)
- Weekly coaching with Chris (SVHQ)
- Friday pitch sessions
- Fundraising access through SVHQ network
- Online mentoring for Agentic Rise

### 3. 🚀 Agentic Rise (Execution Layer)
- 5-10 startups in GenTech Rise programme
- Chris Perelta
- Each gets Business Ghost + VM + Agent OS
- Connected to Execution Exchange for global projects

### 4. ⚡ Super Agents (Local Delivery)
- 1-2 top Rwandan tech companies
- Private VMs, AI workforce, local distribution
- Enterprise sales and government contracts
